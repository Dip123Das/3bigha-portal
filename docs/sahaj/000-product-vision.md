# 000 — Product Vision

## Project SAHAJ

**Human First. AI Second. Precision Always.**

Project SAHAJ is the Human-First product architecture for 3Bigha.

3Bigha is not only a property portal. It is a unified digital platform for land, construction, real estate, procurement, services, marketplace intelligence, geography intelligence and business growth.

## Mission

Make every land, construction, property, procurement and service workflow simple enough for first-time users, while keeping professional-grade intelligence and precision in the background.

## Product Principle

Users should not need to understand software, AI, RFQ, marketplace intelligence, LGD, GIS or procurement systems.

The platform should understand the user's need and prepare the right workflow.

## AI Philosophy

AI should be invisible by default for normal users.

AI may become visible for expert users, administrators and operational workflows where it improves decision-making.

## Location Philosophy

Every location-sensitive workflow should use the same standard:

LGD hierarchy → PIN intelligence → Coordinates → Google Maps → Nearby engine → Marketplace intelligence.

## Engineering Rule

No major user-facing workflow should be redesigned in isolation.

Every redesign must align with:
- the relevant human journey,
- the product domain,
- the shared SAHAJ foundation,
- the universal location engine,
- and the principle: Human First. AI Second. Precision Always.
